# Privacy Policy for Auto Refresh

**Last updated:** November 16, 2026

## 1. Overview
Auto Refresh ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Google Chrome extension handles user data.

## 2. Data Collection and Storage
* **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, transmit, or sell your browsing history, personal information, or webpage content.
* **Local Storage Only:** Extension settings, active timer states, anonymous device identifiers (UUID), and usage counters are stored strictly locally on your device using the Chrome Storage API (`chrome.storage.local` and `chrome.storage.session`). This data never leaves your browser.

## 3. Permissions Justification
* `tabs`: Used solely to reload active or selected tabs and display remaining timer countdowns on the extension badge.
* `alarms`: Used to schedule reliable background refresh intervals.
* `storage`: Used to save your preferred timer intervals and extension settings locally.

## 4. Third-Party Services & Payments
In future versions offering optional paid features, payment processing may be handled by third-party providers (such as Stripe or ExtensionPay). We do not store or process your credit card details on our servers.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
