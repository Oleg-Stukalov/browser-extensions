# Privacy Policy for Auto Refresh

**Last Updated:** November 23, 2026

## 1. Overview

Auto Refresh (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our browser extension handles your information.

**The short version:** Auto Refresh works completely locally in your browser. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your personal data.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

Because Auto Refresh operates strictly on your local device:

* We **do not** collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or IP address).
* We **do not** track your browsing history or monitor the websites you visit.
* We **do not** use third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising scripts.
* We **do not** transmit any webpage content or user activity to external servers.

## 3. Local Data Storage

To function properly, Auto Refresh saves your preferences, refresh intervals, and extension state locally on your device using your browser’s built-in storage API.

* This data remains strictly on your device (or within your personal browser profile if you have browser sync enabled).
* We have no access to this data.
* You can delete this data at any time by clearing your browser’s extension data or uninstalling Auto Refresh.

## 4. Browser Permissions

Auto Refresh requests only the minimum browser permissions required to perform its core auto-refresh features. Any information accessed through these permissions is processed exclusively in real time on your device and is never recorded or sent to external servers.

## 5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user data with third parties, data brokers, or advertisers, as no user data is ever collected by us.

## 6. Compliance with Store Policies

Our data practices comply with the developer policies of all supported browser extension stores, including the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy (Limited Use requirements), Firefox Add-on Policies, and Microsoft Edge Add-ons Policies.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update Auto Refresh in the future in a way that changes how data is handled, we will update this Privacy Policy and revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page before releasing the update.
